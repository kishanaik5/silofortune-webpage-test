About Silo Fortune

At Silo Fortune Pvt Ltd, we are on a mission to revolutionize the dairy and agriculture industry in India. Through innovative technology, sustainable practices, and farmer-centric solutions, we empower Indian farmers to achieve long-term success and profitability.

Our flagship brand, Gau Sampurna, is dedicated to improving cattle health and dairy farming efficiency. With cutting-edge AI tools like the AI-Powered Cattle Health Scanner, we provide farmers with actionable insights to optimize milk production, detect diseases early, and enhance herd management.
