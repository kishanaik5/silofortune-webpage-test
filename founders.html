Our Vision

Why We Started Silo Fortune

A shared dream to revolutionize Indian dairy farming through technology.

"Growing up in rural India, we witnessed firsthand the challenges farmers face—unpredictable yields, cattle diseases, and lack of access to modern knowledge. We founded Silo Fortune with a simple belief: technology should work for farmers, not the other way around.

Through Gau Sampurna and our AI-powered tools, we're bridging the gap between cutting-edge research and ground-level implementation. Our goal is to reach 1 million farmers by 2027, helping them achieve prosperity through sustainable and data-driven practices.

Every farmer's success is our success. That's the Silo Fortune promise."

— The Founding Team
